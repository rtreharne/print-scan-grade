How item statistics are calculated

The master page is excluded. All other scanned pages are included, even before student matching; roster students without scans are excluded. Each question earns one mark when it matches the master. Blank or unreadable responses earn no mark in total scores and are excluded from item percentages. Duplicate student scans remain separate observations.
