Authorised use

You confirm that you are authorised by your institution to create, scan and grade the assessment materials you use here. You must protect student information, follow your institution’s assessment, data-protection and retention policies, and use only answer sheets and materials you are entitled to use.

Where your data goes

In the standalone scan workflow, uploaded PDFs, imported rosters, extracted responses, annotations and grades are processed and retained in your browser. PrintScanGrade stores the answer-sheet design and the contact and acceptance details submitted for this workspace. Do not rely on browser storage as your sole record and export results before clearing browser data or changing device.

Your assessment responsibility

PrintScanGrade is an assistive marking tool, not an authoritative assessment record. You and your institution are solely responsible for checking the scanned pages, answer key, student matching, marking rules, exported data and every grade before it is released, entered into a gradebook or used to make an academic decision.
