From paper to results

Three steps. One workspace.

Start with an answer sheet, then follow the same Print, Scan and Grade steps for each assessment.

  1. 01

    Print

    Name your assessment, choose 1–60 questions and 2–8 answer choices, and add an optional university logo.

    Download the A4 answer sheet and student guide. Complete a master sheet with the correct answers, using student number 000000000 for a generated answer key.

  2. 02

    Scan

    Scan the completed papers to PDF. Upload one multi-page PDF or a ZIP containing several PDFs. Generated student-guide pages are skipped automatically.

    Import a student roster CSV in standalone mode, or use your course roster through LTI. The scanner reads the answer bubbles and attempts to match nine-digit student IDs.

  3. 03

    Grade

    Select the master answer sheet and check the extracted responses. Student scores and percentages are calculated against the key.

    Resolve unmatched students, inspect question analysis, and download the results as CSV. Export the analysis through the browser’s Save as PDF option.

Annotated student review

Open a student’s page to compare detected responses with the answer key and the original scan. Check faint marks, corrections and uncertain responses before relying on the grades.

Quick roster matching

Sort students by name, score or percentage. For unidentified pages, inspect the scanned name, search the roster, and press Enter to assign and advance. Skip pages you want to return to later.

Question and cohort analysis

Explore score distributions, percentage correct by question, upper/lower 27% item discrimination, and answer-option choices. Summary statistics and UG/PG grade classes help you interpret the cohort’s results.

Results you can take away

Download a results CSV for your records or a separate grade-import workflow. Produce a landscape analysis PDF for review and reporting. Automatic gradebook passback is not currently available.

Know where your work lives

Scans and grades stay in your browser.

The browser scan workflows read uploaded PDFs and ZIPs locally. Scan results, page previews, imported rosters and manual matches are saved in that browser. Normal scanning does not send your scan files or grades to the server.

The server stores answer-sheet designs, logos and LTI registrations. In LTI mode, it fetches the course roster from your learning platform to support student matching.

Export your results before clearing browser data. Switching browser, device or site address does not transfer saved scans.

Before your first scan

Which files and sheets can I use?

Generated sheets support 1–60 questions and 2–8 choices. Upload PDFs up to 64 MB each, or a ZIP up to 256 MB. The dedicated UoL legacy reader supports up to 120 questions on the existing A–E form, including colour and grayscale scans.

How do I supply the correct answers?

Complete and scan a master answer sheet, then select it as the key in Grade or Results. For generated sheets, use student ID 000000000. The master is excluded from student grades. In the regular workspace, you can choose marking rules for questions with multiple keyed answers.

What if a student cannot be identified?

Use the unidentified-student dialog to inspect the name area and assign a roster student. Press Enter to assign and continue, or Skip to leave that page unassigned for later. Student matching works with nine-digit numbers. Handwriting without corresponding ID bubble marks may need manual review.

Can I try the workflow before scanning an assessment?

In a regular workspace, import or load a roster and use the dummy ZIP option to generate an answer key and example student sheets. Scan that ZIP to practise matching, review, grading and analysis.
